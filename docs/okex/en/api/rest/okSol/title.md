## SOL staking  

By staking SOL tokens and delegating them to validators on the Solana network, you can receive equivalent OKSOL and earn extra OKSOL rewards.<br>
Stake SOL on Solana to receive OKSOL at a 1:1 ratio for liquidity<br>
