## OKUSD

OKUSD is OKX's stablecoin receipt issued at a 1:1 rate against USDT. Holders earn daily APR with no action required, and OKUSD can be used as trading margin to improve capital efficiency.<br>
Subscription and redemption operate on the funding account. Use the `/limits` endpoint to check your remaining daily quota before subscribing or redeeming.
