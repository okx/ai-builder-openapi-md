## ETH staking  

ETH Staking, also known as Ethereum Staking, is the process of participating in the Ethereum blockchain's Proof-of-Stake (PoS) consensus mechanism.<br>
Stake to receive BETH for liquidity at 1:1 ratio and earn daily BETH rewards<br>
